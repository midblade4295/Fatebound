# Fatebound: what the game is

Every fact here is checked against the game itself (version 0.31.200). If something isn't on this page or in
`champions.md`, the game doesn't have it as far as you know. Don't post it.

## In one line
Fatebound is a fast 5v5 hero battle game (a MOBA) made for phones: pick a champion, push down the lanes and knock
their King off his throne.

## In two lines (store short description)
Fast 5v5 hero battles! Pick a champion, push the lanes and topple their King.

## In a paragraph
Their King sits on a very comfortable chair. Knock him off it. Fatebound is a fast 5v5 hero battle game made for your
phone. Pick one of 18 champions, team up with four others, push down three lanes past the enemy towers and topple their
King before they topple yours. Every match is a fresh fight of up to 20 minutes.

## How a match plays
- **5 against 5** on the Rift: three lanes, three towers in each lane, then the gates, then two more towers guarding the
  King on his throne in the keep.
- **The goal:** knock their King down. He can only be hurt once both of his King towers have fallen. He's large,
  grumpy, and hits back.
- **Up to 20 minutes.** If time runs out, the side that did more damage to the other King wins (then towers, then
  knockouts).
- **Minion waves** march down every lane. Land the last hit and the gold is yours.
- **Jungle camps** between the lanes give gold, experience and buffs: the **Arcane Wraith** (your abilities and
  ultimate recharge faster) and the **Ember Brute** (you hit harder).
- **Two epic monsters:**
  - **The Grave Wyrm**: a huge skeletal dragon in the east pit. It wakes at 3:00 and breathes soul-fire. Each time a
    side slays it (up to four times), all of that side's heroes get tougher for the rest of the match (Wyrm's Hide).
  - **The Bone Lord**: rises in the west pit at 5:00. Bring him down and your side gets gold, experience and his
    buff, your minions come out stronger, and he marches down the middle lane for your side.
- **Level up** during the match (to level 10) and pick an **upgrade card** at each level.
- **Gold and items:** buy items in the shop and build them into bigger ones.
- **HOME:** tap it to teleport back to your castle (6 seconds) and heal at its fountain.
- **Wards** let you see through the fog of war.
- **Every knockout pays, and every death feeds the other team.** First Blood, multi-kills, shutdowns, Aces and
  bounties.

## The champions
- **18 champions in six roles:** tanks, fighters, assassins, mages, marksmen and supports. Each one has a name, a
  title, a story and a line of their own (see `champions.md`).
- Every champion has **three abilities** (unlocked at levels 1, 2 and 3) and an **ultimate** at level 6, which gets
  stronger at level 9.
- **12 champions are yours from the start.** The other 6 (Golem, Dragoon, Duelist, Stormcaller, Musketeer, Druid) are
  unlocked in the game, and each week two of those six are in a **rotation everyone can play**.

## Ways to play
- **Online 5v5 matches**, with bots filling any empty seats.
- **A match against bots** whenever you like.
- **Party up** with friends and clanmates, up to five together.
- **The Royal Herald** teaches you the ropes in a funny, fully voiced tutorial.

## Clans
- Choose your **crest** and grow your clan's **camp** from a single tent to a great hall (10 levels, 4 stages).
- Earn clan points in every match for the weekly **Clan Chest**, and climb the **Clan League**.
- Ask your clan for **hero shards**, and give yours to clanmates.
- **Looking For Group** to find players and jump straight into a party.
- **Chat** with your clan, your party and everyone online.

## Collect and customize
- **36 outfits:** a set made for every champion with its own weapon (18), plus the **Frostbound** line (18, one for
  every champion).
- **Outfits are cosmetic only.** They change how a champion looks, never how a champion plays.
- **Chests** full of gold, gems and hero shards.
- The **Siege Pass** (its tiers of prizes), **daily orders**, **champion quests** and **weekly leaderboard** prizes.

## The look and sound
- Bright, chunky 3D fantasy: little heroes, castles, rolling hills, a river ford, golden-hour light.
- Made with the **Godot** game engine. (#MadeWithGodot is fine to use.)
- The Royal Herald is the game's narrator: a dry, very British royal announcer who would rather be napping. He
  voices the tutorial and the trailer.
