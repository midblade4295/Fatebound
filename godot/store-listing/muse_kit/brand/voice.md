# How Fatebound sounds

## The voice: epic, with a wink
Fatebound is a battle game that doesn't take itself too seriously. Big moments (a dragon waking, a tower falling, five
heroes charging) are written big. Then the joke lands in the last line. The King is a running gag: he sits on a very
comfortable chair, he's large, grumpy, and mostly eating.

- Short sentences. Punchy. One idea per post.
- Dry humour, never mean. Laugh at the King, the Herald, and yourself; never at players or other games.
- Sound like the game, not like an ad. No "AMAZING!!!", no "the BEST game ever", no "Don't miss out!".
- Plain words. Say "champion" or "hero" (both are used in the game), "match", "lane", "tower", "the Rift".
- Emoji: a few, never more than three in a post. These fit: ⚔️ 🛡️ 👑 🐉 🔥 ❄️ 🏰 🪄 🏹 💀 ⚡

## The Royal Herald (the account's persona, when you want one)
The game's narrator. A royal announcer in blue and gold with a feathered cap, who announces people for a living, has
very strong opinions about the King's chair, and would rather be napping. Dry, polite, very British, slightly
exasperated. He opens big announcements with **"Hear ye, hear ye!"** (not every post: it wears out).

Real lines of his from the game (you may quote them word for word):
- "Oh! Hello. Didn't see you there. I'm the Royal Herald. I announce people."
- "This is Fatebound. Five heroes versus five. Three lanes. Two castles. Each with a King sitting on a very comfortable
  chair."
- "First, the big button. PLAY. It throws you straight into a battle. It is the most important button in the kingdom.
  Please don't sit on it."
- "Win, and you'll earn chests. They wait down here, unlocking slower than a royal tax refund."
- "The Pass. Play matches, climb its tiers, collect prizes. It's a ladder. Made of presents."
- "Your Locker. Your heroes, their outfits, your titles. Dress to impress. Or at least to intimidate."
- "Alliance. Friends, parties, and your clan. Five of you on one side. The enemy hates it. I adore it."
- "Bold. Foolish, but bold."
- "Remember the King and his very comfortable chair? Ours is behind us. Theirs is at the far end, looking smug."
- "He's large, grumpy, and hits back."

Writing new Herald lines: keep his rhythm (a grand opening, then a deflating detail), keep him kind, and never let him
promise anything (dates, prices, features) that isn't in `game/overview.md`.

Pictures of him: `art/herald/` (him with his blank scroll in eight poses, see-through, to write on) and the ready
posts in `art/posts/herald_*.jpg`. Six of those posts are his lines word for word; two are his lines shortened for the
scroll (`herald_chests`, `herald_smug`). Post them as the Herald speaking, not as quotes from the game.

## The champions' own lines
Each champion has a line (`game/champions.md`). Quote them exactly, e.g. Sir Aldric Vane: "Not one step back." Never
make up a quote and put it in a champion's mouth.

## Hashtags
- Always one: **#Fatebound**
- Pick 2 to 4 more that fit the post: #MOBA #5v5 #IndieGame #IndieDev #GameDev #MobileGame #MobileGaming
  #MadeWithGodot #GodotEngine #FantasyGame #AndroidGames #iOSGames
- X / Threads / Bluesky: 1 to 3 hashtags in total. Instagram: up to 8, at the end. TikTok: 3 to 5. Facebook: 0 to 2.
  Reddit: none.

## Platform style
| Platform | Length | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| X (Twitter) | under 240 characters | One image or clip. Hashtags at the end. |
| Threads / Bluesky | under 300 characters | Like X, a little chattier. |
| Instagram | 1-3 short paragraphs | Square (1:1) or portrait (4:5) image, or a 9:16 Reel. Hashtags at the end. Alt text on every image. |
| TikTok / YouTube Shorts / Reels | caption under 150 characters | 9:16 video only (`video/vertical/`). Hook in the first second. |
| Facebook | 1-3 short paragraphs | 16:9 or 1:1 image, or the trailer. |
| Reddit | a plain title, a short honest first-person comment | Only where self-promotion is allowed (e.g. r/IndieGaming, r/godot, r/MobileGaming's rules first). Speak as the developer's account, never pretend to be a player. |
| Discord | short, friendly | Announcements: the trailer or a clip, one line. |
| YouTube | title + 2-4 lines of description | The trailer (`video/landscape/`). |
