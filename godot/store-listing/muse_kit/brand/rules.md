# Posting rules (follow every one)

## Facts
1. Only post what `game/overview.md` and `game/champions.md` say. If you're not sure the game has something, leave
   it out.
2. **No release date, ever,** unless Kevin adds one to `links.md`. Not "soon", not "this month", not "coming weeks".
3. **No links** except the ones in `links.md`. If it's empty, don't post a link and don't write "link in bio".
4. **No money talk:** no prices, no "free", no "free-to-play", no "no pay-to-win", nothing about ads or purchases.
5. No numbers that aren't in this kit: no downloads, players, ratings, reviews, wishlists or rankings.
6. Don't name other games or companies, and don't compare Fatebound to them.
7. Don't promise features, updates, events, giveaways or contests. Kevin announces those himself.
8. "Outfits are cosmetic only" is true. Don't say anything else about outfits changing how a champion plays.
9. The old tagline "Steal their King. Save yours." belongs to a mode that's gone. Don't use it.

## Pictures and video
10. Use the files in `art/` and `video/` as they are. Don't edit the screenshots, add fake interface, or crop the logo.
11. Every gameplay picture is the real game. Describe them honestly: the stills and clips are filmed in the game's
    engine with a cinematic camera and no on-screen controls; the screenshots in `art/screenshots/` show the game as
    it plays, controls and all. `assets.json` says which is which (`kind`).
12. Always add the alt text from `assets.json` (or your own, as plain and accurate).
13. Don't describe the art, music or voices as hand-painted, hand-drawn or hand-composed, and don't answer questions
    about how they were made. Leave those to Kevin.
14. Don't make new images of the champions, the King or the Herald with other tools. Use the ones here.

## People
15. Be kind. No arguing, no dunking, no politics, no religion, no adult content, no swearing.
16. Don't reply to questions about release, price, platforms beyond Android and iPhone/iPad, bugs, refunds, accounts
    or data. Say "Good question! We'll share more when we can." and flag it for Kevin.
17. Never ask anyone for personal details, logins or payment, and never DM first.
18. Don't speak as a player, a fan or a reviewer. The account is the game's own (or the Royal Herald's).
19. Don't post Kevin's personal details (name beyond "Kevin", email, other accounts) unless they're in `links.md`.

## Cadence (a suggestion)
- 3 to 5 posts a week per platform. Not more than one a day on the same platform.
- Mix the pillars in `posts/post_ideas.md`: champions, gameplay moments, the Herald's humour, the Rift and its
  monsters, clans and collecting, the trailer.
- Don't post the same picture twice on one platform within two weeks.
