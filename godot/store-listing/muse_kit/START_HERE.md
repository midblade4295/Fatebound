# Fatebound social kit: start here

This folder is everything needed to post about **Fatebound** on social media: what the game is, how it sounds, what
you may and may not say, ready-made posts, and the pictures and videos to go with them.

**Fatebound** is a fast 5v5 hero battle game (a MOBA) made for phones. Pick one of 18 champions, team up with four
others, push down three lanes past the enemy towers and knock their King off his very comfortable chair. It isn't out
yet and has no release date.

## Read these first, in this order
1. `brand/rules.md`: the rules. Follow every one, every time.
2. `game/overview.md`: what the game is, and what it isn't.
3. `brand/voice.md`: how to sound (and the Royal Herald, the game's narrator).
4. `links.md`: the only links you may post (empty for now).

## Then use
- `posts/post_ideas.md`: ready posts with the files to attach (also as `posts/posts.json`), and a plan for the first
  two weeks.
- `game/champions.md`: the 18 champions: names, titles, stories, lines and abilities (also in `game/facts.json`).
- `assets.json`: every picture and video: what it shows, alt text to use, and which platforms it fits.
- `brand/profiles.md`: profile pictures, banners and bios for each platform.

## What's in `art/` and `video/`
| Folder | What |
|---|---|
| `art/logo/` | The FATEBOUND logo, see-through PNG |
| `art/icon/` | The game's icon at profile-picture sizes |

| `art/outfits/` | Each champion's epic outfit, a card each |

| `art/gameplay_stills/` | Ten 16:9 moments from the trailer: the Rift, the King, the charge, abilities, the Grave Wyrm |
| `art/herald/` | The Royal Herald with a blank scroll in eight poses, see-through, to write on |
| `video/landscape/` | The trailer (47 s) and five clips of it, 16:9 |
| `video/vertical/` | The same, 9:16, for TikTok, Reels and Shorts |

Made from the game itself (version noted in `game/champions.md`). If the game changes, Kevin will send a new kit.
