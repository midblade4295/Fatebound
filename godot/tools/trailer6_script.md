# Fatebound trailer 6 -- "Anyone Can Change Fate"

Kevin's brief: an epic cinematic trailer that follows a villager through a battle, with the theme "everyone makes a
difference". Shoved aside while the others put on their helmets, he finds the bomb and runs it across the battlefield.
An enemy Archmage hurls a meteor onto his friends and knocks him down (shown from the Archmage's hand, so it's
clear the blast isn't his bomb). He gets up, his allies rally round him, and he throws the bomb at their gate. The
narrator speaks only at the key moments.

Staged in `tools/trailer6_shots.gd` (four runs: court, run, fall, charge), cut and mixed in `tools/trailer6_edit.py`,
narration and music in `tools/trailer6_vo/raw/` (ElevenLabs). The title is trailer 4's gold "golden" shot.
