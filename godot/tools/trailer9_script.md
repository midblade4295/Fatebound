# Fatebound trailer 9 -- the CGI trailer (plan, for Kevin's OK)

Kevin: "Was thinking of doing a full trailer with cgi" -- yes to a shot list and Herald script first, then one test
shot.

A 60-second cinematic: AI-generated video (ElevenLabs flows) in the game's own look made film-grade -- the chunky,
friendly heroes, the King and the Grave Wyrm as they are in the game, with movie lighting, materials, smoke and fire.
Every shot starts from the game's own pictures (the champion renders, the King, the Wyrm, the castles), first turned
into a cinematic keyframe (an image edit), then animated, so the champions stay on-model. The Royal Herald narrates
(most of his lines are already recorded for trailer 8; two are new), the score is trailer 8's, and it ends on the
game's real animated title. A small "Not actual gameplay footage" note sits at the foot of the title card (for social
media and YouTube; the store pages keep trailer 8, which is the real game).

Story: the running joke and the epic together -- their King on his very comfortable chair, our five going to knock him
off it, the battle on the way, the Grave Wyrm waking, and the King, mid-bite, seeing them come in.

## The test shot (#9)
The game's Wyrm still (art_sources/trailer9/refs/wyrm_still.jpg, from trailer 8's recording) -> a cinematic keyframe
(gpt-image-2.5-sunburst edit, the creature and the pit kept) -> 8 s of video (Veo 3.1 Fast). On the flow "Fatebound
trailer 9 (CGI)".

Made (Kevin: "Yes"): two keyframes (866 credits; the second picked: the Wyrm bigger in frame) and the 8 s clip
(7,271): 8,137 credits ($1.48) in all. The Wyrm turns to the lens, spreads its wings, stalks forward and roars, a
burst of green soul-fire filling the frame. On-model enough to read as the Grave Wyrm, but the video model adds bone
detail the game's faceted Wyrm doesn't have. Its own sound (bone creaks, wing beat, roar) peaks at -1 dB. Waiting on
Kevin's verdict before any more shots.
