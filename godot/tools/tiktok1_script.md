# Fatebound TikTok 1 -- "The King's snack"

Kevin: "I want a short CGI video that will make people on tiktok want to watch right off the bat. Something that could get
a lot of views on tiktok" -- picked "King's snack" (a comedy loop) at about 15 s.

A vertical 9:16 short (1080 x 1920, 30 fps, -14 LUFS) built to hold a scroller from the first frame and to loop:
- **0.0 s** -- the hook is on screen at once: an extreme close-up of the round King mid-bite on a giant turkey leg, and
  the caption **"POV: you're the boss in a 5v5"** (TikTok's style, high in the frame, clear of the app's tabs, buttons
  and caption).
- **~1.5 s** -- BOOM: the throne hall's doors blow in off-screen; light and splinters hit him; he freezes mid-chew.
- **3.4 s** -- his point of view, over his shoulder: the five heroes charge at him down the red carpet through the dust.
- **6.4 s** -- caught: frozen, his crown slides off and clangs, one huge GULP, then he sheepishly offers them the turkey leg.
- **12.0 s** -- the game's title slams in (trailer 8's cue B hitting with the game's title slam), 5V5 HERO BATTLES, "Not
  actual gameplay footage".
- **13.9 s** -- the loop: straight back to him happily biting, so a viewer sees the start again before they've scrolled.

The shots are AI video (ElevenLabs flow "Fatebound TikTok 1 (King's snack)", Veo 3.1 Fast, 9:16 at 720p, their own sound
effects), each started from a vertical keyframe (gpt-image-2.5-sunburst) made from trailer 9's own frames of the King
(shots 2 and 14) and of the five heroes in the breach (shot 13, the archer's bow lowered), so they match trailer 9. Cut and
mixed by tools/tiktok1_edit.py (--clean: no caption, for TikTok's own text).

  app.
