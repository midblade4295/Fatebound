# Squadron Skies 5v5 (working title)

Stylized 5v5 WWII arcade dogfighting for landscape phones, built in Godot 4
(GDScript, Mobile renderer) with a Clash-Royale-style card progression loop.

This folder is a **stand-alone Godot project**. It shares nothing with the Siege
project in `../godot`; open `dogfight/project.godot` directly.

- Engine: written and tested on Godot **4.7.2**. Uses typed dictionaries and
  physics interpolation, so 4.4 is the practical minimum.
- Flow: `MainMenu` (Hangar) -> `MatchLobby` -> `DogfightArena5v5` -> Hangar.
- Offline today: 1 player + 9 bots. The scene/script split is laid out for
  `MultiplayerSpawner` / `MultiplayerSynchronizer`, but **no online session code
  exists yet and nothing has been tested with a second peer**.

Reference captures (software-rendered at 1280x720): `docs/arena_hud.png`,
`docs/hangar.png`, `docs/spitfire_model.png`.

## Controls

| Action | Touch | Keyboard (desktop testing) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Steer (turn / pitch, auto-bank) | left-thumb floating stick | WASD or arrows |
| Fire | FIRE button (hold) | Space |
| Evade roll | ROLL button | Shift or Q |
| Tactical gadget | gadget button (radial cooldown) | E |

Stick up = nose up. `Profile.invert_pitch` flips it. Mouse clicks act as touches
on desktop (`emulate_touch_from_mouse`), so the on-screen controls work there too.

## How the pieces fit

- **A plane does not know who flies it.** `PlaneActor` reads its `PlaneInput`
  child each physics tick. `PlayerController` and `SquadAIController` only write
  to that node, so the match manager can swap a seat between a human and a bot
  (`assign_human` / `release_to_ai`) without touching the plane.
- **Input goes through actions.** The touch stick and buttons press the same
  Input actions the keyboard does; `PlayerController` never references a widget.
- **Bullets are data.** `BulletSystem` simulates every bullet in arrays, sweeps
  one ray per bullet per tick and draws them all with one MultiMesh. A bullet's
  ray excludes the shooter's whole team, which is how friendly fire is prevented
  (`PlaneActor.apply_damage` rejects it again as a backstop).
- **One lead solution.** `Ballistics.lead_point` is used by the HUD reticle, the
  player's aim assist and the bots, and matches how bullets are actually fired
  (they inherit the plane's velocity).
- **Match rules** (`MatchManager5v5`): ticket drain, every loss gives the other
  team a point, first to 25 or the higher score after 3 minutes, 5 s respawn
  above the team's base with 2.5 s spawn protection.
- **Damage is per part.** Besides the hull, a plane has an engine, two wings and
  a tail, each with its own smaller health pool. `PlaneActor.locate_hit` decides
  which one a bullet struck; `apply_damage` always hurts the hull and, for a
  part hit, that part too. What damage does:

  | Part | As it takes damage | Destroyed |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | Wing | wider turns, drags and hangs toward that side, slow to turn away from it, sluggish stick, extra drag | spins in, breaks up within 2.6 s |
  | Tail | weak pitch and rudder, sluggish stick, nose-heavy past half damage | almost no pitch control, dives |
  | Engine | lower top speed, slow to regain speed | on fire, glides, stalls unless it dives |

  Whoever destroys a part is credited with the takedown however the plane then
  dies. The Squad Repair Pulse patches damaged parts but not destroyed ones.
  The HUD shows the player's parts on a small diagram under the commander panel
  and names damaged parts under the current target.
- **Bots** are tuned to fly like people: they aim at where the target was a
  reaction time ago (so jinking throws them off), their aim wanders, they
  misjudge lead, their stick movement is rate-limited and they fire in bursts.
  In bots-only test matches about 14% of their shots land. They pick targets by score (range, angle off the nose, low health) with a
  penalty for targets team-mates already claimed, approach in personal lanes,
  push apart when close, avoid the sea and cliffs, and break/roll when shot
  from behind.

## Progression

- `ComponentCard` (Resource): Engine / Armament / Airframe / Gadget, four
  rarities. Rarer cards start at a higher level (Common 1, Rare 3, Epic 6,
  Legendary 9) and all cap at 13. Each level multiplies the card's bonuses by
  `1 + growth_per_level`. Upgrades cost coins (by level) and spare copies (by
  how many upgrades the card has had) and award Hangar XP.
- `CommanderProgression`: Commander Level is derived from lifetime Hangar XP.
  Each level adds +7% HP and +1% speed to every plane, unlocks planes
  (Lv1 Hurricane, Lv3 Spitfire, Lv6 Mustang) and opens card slots
  (Engine, Armament, Gadget at Lv1; Airframe at Lv4; Wildcard at Lv8).
- `PlaneData.build_stats()` = base stats x Commander Level x equipped cards,
  producing the `PlaneStats` a plane flies with.
- `Profile` (autoload) holds coins, XP, card levels/copies and the loadout and
  saves them to `user://squadron_profile.json`. Local and cosmetic only.

All tuning numbers (costs, XP, damage, turn rates) are first-pass values.

## Look

- `shaders/toon_plane.gdshader`: banded diffuse, hard specular blob, light-aware
  rim plus a small ambient rim. `ToonMaterials.standard_toon()` is the
  StandardMaterial3D equivalent (toon diffuse/specular + built-in rim) for
  meshes that need textures.
- `shaders/ocean.gdshader` and `cloud.gdshader` are fragment-only and read no
  screen or depth textures.
- Lighting: warm `Sun` with shadows, cool unshadowed `RimLight` from the
  opposite side, sky ambient, light fog, mild glow, +20% saturation.
- Planes, islands, the carrier and clouds are primitive meshes built in code
  (`plane_visual.gd`, `arena_builder.gd`). `PlaneVisual` is the single place to
  swap in authored models.

## Authored plane model

`tools/blender/spitfire_stylized.py` is a Blender 4.x script that models a
chunky Spitfire and exports `assets/models/spitfire_stylized.glb`:

```
blender --background --python tools/blender/spitfire_stylized.py -- assets/models/spitfire_stylized.glb
```

The model faces -Z. `SpitfireBody/PropellerMesh` has its origin on the hub, so
`rotate_z()` spins it. Surfaces are named `BodyPrimary`,
`WingAccent_TeamColor`, `CanopyGlass`, `MetalDark` and `PropellerWood`;
override `WingAccent_TeamColor` to repaint a team. It is **not wired into
`PlaneVisual` yet**: the game still flies the primitive planes.

Preview it with `res://tools/ModelPreview.tscn`.

## Android test build

```
GODOT=/path/to/godot ANDROID_HOME=/path/to/android-sdk tools/build_android.sh
```

Exports the "Android Preview" preset (`com.squadronskies.preview`, arm64-v8a +
armeabi-v7a, sensor landscape, no permissions) with the release template to
`build/SquadronSkies-preview.apk`. Pass `KEYSTORE`, `KS_ALIAS` and `KS_PASS` to
sign with a key you keep; otherwise a throwaway key is generated in `build/`
(git-ignored), and a build signed with a different key will not install over
it. This is a sideload build for testing, not a store build.

## Tests

```
GODOT=/path/to/godot tools/run_tests.sh            # add --strict to make typing warnings errors
```

| Test | What it checks |
| --- | --- |
| `TestProgression` | card tables, upgrade flow, Commander Level thresholds, slot rules, stat building, save/load, lead maths |
| `TestDamageModel` | hit location, hull vs part damage, each damaged part changes handling against an undamaged reference, destroyed wing/engine/tail bring the plane down and credit the attacker, repair |
| `TestPlayerFlow` | 1 player + 9 bots through Input actions: fire, roll, nitro, crash gives a ticket, 5 s respawn |
| `TestMatchSim` (3 seeds) | full bots-only match: 10 planes, score = losses, zero friendly-fire hits, respawn delay, no clumping, bots do not crash, bot accuracy stays in a human-like 2-30% band |
| hangar / lobby | scenes load and the lobby hands over to the arena without script errors |

These run headless, so they prove rules and wiring, not feel. Screens were also
checked from software-rendered captures (`tools/ShotCapture.tscn`). **Not yet
tested:** a real phone (touch feel, frame rate, thermal; the APK builds and its
signature verifies, but it has not been run on a device) and anything networked.

`project.godot` makes untyped declarations a compile error.
